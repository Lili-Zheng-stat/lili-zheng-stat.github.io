Publications and Preprints

Research interest: I am broadly interested in statistical machine learning, where I develop reliable methods and theory for large-scale and complex statistical problems. In particular, I have been working on the following topics: uncertainty quantification, interpretable machine learning, statistical learning, ensemble methods, data integration, spectral methods, graph learning.
